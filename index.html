"Wake at" includes your time to fall asleep. Early afternoon (roughly 1–3 pm) is the easiest time to nap without hurting the night's sleep.

How this works, honestly

Sleep runs in cycles of light, deep and REM sleep. A cycle is about 90 minutes on average, but it varies from roughly 70 to 120 minutes between people and through the night, so treat these times as a guide, not a rule.

Waking near the end of a cycle, in lighter sleep, tends to feel easier than being pulled out of deep sleep. That's all this calculator aims for.

Total sleep matters most. Most adults need 7 to 9 hours; teenagers need more. Five or six cycles fits that; four is a short night.

Not medical advice. If you're often tired despite enough sleep, talk to a doctor.
